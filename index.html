    How to play 🏓

    1
    🖱️
    Mouse = paddle

    Your player holds a big paddle. It follows your mouse. The camera is behind you: the ball comes towards you.

    2
    🎯
    Your turn? LEFT CLICK!

    When the ball comes to you, a 🎯 target appears. Put your paddle on it and left click (or hold the button): you swing when the ball arrives.

    3
    🔄
    Then switch sides

    After you hit, you run to the other end by yourself, with turbo shoes. Press Space at the end of the table to slide under it 🛝.

    4
    👋
    RIGHT CLICK = slap

    Someone in your way gets a 👋 sign: right click to slap them aside! The player who is hitting is protected 🛡️.

    💔 Miss the ball = lose a heart. 2 hearts each, then you're a ghost. Last one standing wins! · 🙂 In Easy you walk by yourself, so the mouse alone is enough.

    C = change camera (behind you · whole table · first person) · WASD / ZQSD / arrows = run · Right click on the ball = backspin · swing sideways = aim · swing in an arc = curve.
